Add host

As after ssh in a terminal: a Host of ~/.ssh/config, or user@host with -p -l -i -J. Your keys sign in; ssh must not ask for a password.

Its files and its coding agents' progress are set up on the host by themselves (a small helper in ~/.terminum, terminum's hooks in ~/.claude and ~/.codex).

Attach tmux sessions

Sessions of your own tmux there. They stay yours: terminum never closes them, and shows one as soon as it runs.
