# v0.2.0 — your own tmux sessions, a quick shell

## New

- **Your own tmux sessions in terminum.** Press **Attach tmux sessions** under the list, or
  run `terminum tmux attach <session>`. You can attach sessions of the tmux you run yourself,
  on this Mac or on a host (`terminum tmux attach <session> --host <host>`). Running sessions
  appear as ticks. A session with `destroy-unattached` on says that tmux closes it once
  terminum is its last client and lets go. terminum never starts that tmux and never makes
  or closes a session there. Looking never resizes what your own terminals show: only typing
  or **Fit to this screen** does, and terminum gives the sizes back when it lets go.
- **Sessions on a host.** After **Add host** terminum offers the host's other sessions. An
  icon on the host's group, with their count, offers them again later. Each attached session
  is one more ssh connection to the host.
- **Quick shell.** Pick **Quick shell below** or **Quick shell on the left** in the pane's menu to
  open a shell beside the pane, in its folder. It is a tmux window of its own, listed under
  its pane, and not restored after a reboot. Computers only.

## Changed

- **A full-screen program you only look at is read at your text size.** Codex in a session
  that terminum does not resize was scaled down to fit the window. Now, as in iterm-enhancer,
  its lines re-flow at your text size. A program that draws a frame (mc, htop) is still
  scaled to fit, or shown at your size with sideways scrolling when scaling would make it
  smaller than 7 px.
- **Pasted images** go to `~/.terminum/tmp`, on the Mac and on hosts. A TIFF from Preview or
  a HEIC image in Safari is pasted as a PNG.
- **A file made with New File…** opens in View, where a click on a file opens it.
- **The interface is consistent:**
  - header buttons, dialog buttons and menus share one look;
  - buttons on a phone are big enough for a finger;
  - a long message wraps inside the screen;
  - a waiting card no longer covers the banners.

  The rules are in `docs/UI-GUIDELINES.md`.
- **A host's group** offers no **+ New window** until the host is connected.

## Upgrading

`terminum upgrade`. Nothing to migrate: sessions you attach are kept in `settings.json`
(`attach`, and `hosts.<name>.attach`).

## Validation

The following pass on the released tree:

- Rust unit tests and `cargo clippy` with no warnings; UI, page and script unit tests
  (`make test`).
- The protocol checks against a private terminumd and tmux server: core, access, restore,
  sizing, remote hosts, your own sessions here and on a host (stand-in ssh).
- The page's browser checks in Chromium and WebKit, at a wide and a phone size, including
  layout checks. The Files panel's browser checks, and the live checks against a private tmux
  server, including paste and the quick shell.
- `scripts/security_check.sh`, and the installer's check in a temporary home.

Not checked: a real sshd for remote hosts, including attaching a host's own sessions; the
upgrade from v0.1.0 on another Mac.
