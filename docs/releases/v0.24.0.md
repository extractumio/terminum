# v0.24.0 — the agent's records as bookmarks, going through bookmarks, long paths shown short

A minor release: a new feature (a coding agent keeps its reports, findings and questions as
your bookmarks and reminders, replacing the Plan pane's notes; going through a session's
bookmarks) and a changed MCP tool. Read **Before you upgrade** first.

## New

- **The agent keeps what must not get lost in the scroll.** A coding agent hands you a final
  report, a next step, a gap it saw elsewhere in the project, deferred work or a question
  through terminum's `add_notes_for_user`, and each becomes a bookmark of its session with the
  agent's mark: a title and a text written to read without the chat (Markdown). It is a
  reminder, coming up at each turn's end, only when you must act or decide. Open goes to where
  the agent made it (the line where its call was printed, found by its title); Insert into
  chat pastes its title and text. The agent's records have limits of their own (50 open a
  session or project, 500 in all) and never count against your 300 bookmarks; the same title
  is not kept twice; the text is cleaned and redacted as sent history is. Seen with Claude
  Code: a task asking for a final report kept it as a bookmark, a choice left to the user
  became a question, a routine edit kept nothing.
- **Going through a session's bookmarks.** The bookmarks' button (its count: bookmarks and open
  reminders) lists the session's bookmarks and open reminders top to bottom, the agent's with
  its mark and reminders with a bell. Press one, or ↑ ↓ on a computer, and the session goes
  there while the list stays open; ✓ or Delete settles one (a reminder resolved, a bookmark
  deleted) with Undo for 5 seconds; Enter or Esc closes it. The bar over the terminal has the
  same Done.
- **The Plan pane in sections**: a larger title, "Now" with the current step set apart, the
  progress as "3 of 9 done", a percent and a bar with the ETA under it, the steps with round
  marks and the current one highlighted, when it was updated, and how many records the agent
  kept for you (a press opens the bookmarks' list). It takes the theme's text colors, so it
  reads in light themes too.
- **A long path shows short.** A path of 84 characters or more in a row of output shows its
  first 36 and last 40 characters with "[...]" between; a click or a tap (or Enter) on
  "[...]" shows it whole while the page is open, and Find opens it when a match is in its
  middle. Copy, ⌘-click and underlines always take the whole path. Not folded in the cursor's
  row, an agent's input box or footer, or a full-screen program; a path broken over two rows
  is shown as it is.

## Before you upgrade

- **The Plan pane's notes become bookmarks.** At its first start, terminumd turns the open
  notes of `~/.terminum/state/notes.json` into the agent's bookmarks of their sessions and
  projects, once, and keeps the file as `notes.json.imported`; done notes are not carried over.
  Your own bookmarks are not touched.
- **`add_notes_for_user` changed**: a record is a `title` and a `text` (both required), with
  `remind`, `kind` (bug, deferred, followup, question, report) and `file`. Restart running
  Claude Code and Codex sessions: they read terminum's tools and their descriptions only when
  they start, and an old session's call in the old shape is refused as a tool error ("notes[0].title is
  required"), nothing kept.
- **Rolling back** to 0.23 keeps the agent's records as bookmarks, but 0.23 does not know
  they are the agent's: they lose the agent's mark and count against your 300. The Plan pane of 0.23 shows no notes (`notes.json` was imported);
  rename `notes.json.imported` back to `notes.json` before rolling back to keep them.

## Fixed

- **No notice for Claude Code's question in a narrow pane.** A session waiting on its
  question (AskUserQuestion) showed no question mark, no waiting line and sent no notice
  after 2 minutes when its pane was narrow (an agent team's panes beside it): Claude Code
  breaks "Esc to cancel" over two rows there, and terminum read the screen as finished. The
  footer's words are now read as one line.
- **Image buttons alone on a line.** The image buttons after a row as wide as the view stood
  alone on the line below it; they stay with the row's last word.
- **A warning in every Linux build** (zig's linker on rustc's `-Wl,-O1`): cargo-zigbuild
  0.24.0 filters it out. Building from source: `make toolchain` once; an older cargo-zigbuild
  is refused with that advice.
