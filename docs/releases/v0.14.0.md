# v0.14.0 — Marks of the whole chat, Ask, and Insert into chat

## Added

- **Marks on the terminal's right edge** (a wide window): where your messages (white), the
  agent's answers ended (pale blue), bookmarks (orange) and open reminders (pink) stand in the
  history. The edge is the whole history terminum keeps (12,000 lines by default): marks move up
  as the chat grows and go once their lines are dropped. Press one to go there. Settings' **Side
  marks** turns them off in this browser.
- **Ask**: select text in the terminal and press the blue speech bubble under the orange [+];
  type a question and **Add to Chat** puts the selection quoted as "Quote #0007", your question
  and a rule into the input, set off by a blank line, without Enter. The number counts up per
  session, on every device.
- **The sent history's pane**: **Jump to message** shows where it was sent in this session's
  history; **Insert into chat** types it again, without Enter.
- **Insert into chat** on a bookmark's bar and a reminder's card: its note and its text quoted.

## Changed

- **Bookmarks / Reminders** below the sessions opens a large window on the right (the whole
  width on a phone) instead of a box in the session list.
- **The floating buttons** start a little lower and stand further apart; each pane opens a
  little further from its button, its first row level with it; the magnifier stays in sight,
  ringed, while its field is open; the header's ⋯ stands right above them.
- **The terminal's text** stops a little before the marks on its right edge.
- **The reminders' bell** blinks only on a touch screen; on a computer it stands still with its
  count filled. The list's Bookmarks / Reminders button shows an orange bookmark and a pink
  bell.
- **The list**: a project's group has as much space under its last session as above its name;
  the unread mark stands on the program's line; the header's bar no longer breathes (the list's
  still does while an agent works).
- **Claude Code** is named "claude" in the list, not by its version ("2.1.304").

## Fixed

- **A false ? on a session**: text that reads like a question ("Would you like to…") printed in
  Claude Code's transcript made a session waiting when terminum read its screen.
- **A waiting session's ? stayed** a few seconds after it was opened and answered; it now goes
  as soon as the session is shown.
