# v0.7.0 — Codex as Claude Code, and a split view

## Changed

- **Codex's input is a box, as Claude Code's.** Codex draws its prompt (`›`) on a background
  of its own, without rules. The page now finds it: on a computer it stands between two thin
  lines, on a phone it is a panel with its status line (model · folder · branch · state ·
  context) behind ⓘ, also while Codex works. Taps, ↑ ↓ and a held finger's slide move its
  cursor as in Claude Code's. Codex draws on the alternate screen, which used to switch the page
  to Screen mode; a coding agent's own full screen now stays in Lines mode ("Always" still
  gives the grid).
- **Codex installed with npm is known as Codex** (it runs as `node`): its mark in the list and
  the header, and its states (working, waiting for an answer, failed) as Codex 0.162 shows them.
- **Split the view with ⌘D and ⌘⇧D**, as in iTerm2: a terminal of its own on the right (⌘D) or
  below (⌘⇧D, or the new button under the magnifier at the terminal's top right). It is the
  pane's quick shell, listed under its pane; the browser's own ⌘D (bookmark) does not come. The
  quick shell can now also stand on the right (the menu: "Quick shell on the right").
- **A working session's bar pulses**, in the list and in the header: its color glows and
  fades. It is a state, not motion, so it shows with macOS's Reduce Motion on too.
- **The text size follows the theme.** The menu's − and + now keep a step on the theme's size
  (or settings.json's `page.textSize`), so a theme's new size shows; a size an older page kept
  is dropped once. The default themes' 15 shows now where an older page had kept its own size.
  On a phone the text is 14 at most (was 13).

## Fixed

- **An agent's footer on a phone waits behind ⓘ again** while the agent works and you type:
  lines it shows only then (a background shell, its tokens) no longer stay in sight. Below an
  agent's input every footer line waits behind ⓘ but a `/` or `@` list the input opened.
- **A Claude Code pane whose window you named shows Claude Code's mark.** Claude Code's process
  is named by its version (`2.1.302`), which the list did not know as Claude Code.
- **A quick shell on a phone is one tap away:** the button under the magnifier shows the pane's
  quick shell, and on it leads back to its pane.

## Upgrading

`terminum upgrade`. Nothing to migrate. If the text looks too big or too small after the
upgrade, press − or + once in the menu: the step is remembered in that browser.

## Validation

The following pass on the released tree:

- Rust unit tests and `cargo clippy` with no warnings; UI, page and script unit tests
  (`make test`).
- The protocol checks against a private terminumd and tmux server: core, access, restore,
  sizing, the WebSocket, remote hosts, your own sessions here and on a host (stand-in ssh),
  and the agents' states.
- The page's browser checks in Chromium and WebKit, at a wide and a phone size, with new
  checks for Codex's box, ⌘D and ⌘⇧D, the pulse and the text size. The Files panel's browser
  checks, and the live checks against a private tmux server.
- `scripts/security_check.sh`, and the installer's check in a temporary home.

Not checked: a real sshd for remote hosts; an iPhone or iPad itself; Codex's box with a long,
multi-line input on a live Codex (the checks use the shape measured from Codex 0.16x in tmux);
⌘D in Safari (checked in Chromium and WebKit through Playwright, where the page prevents it).
