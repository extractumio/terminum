# v0.12.0 — Bookmarks of their project, a keyboard only when asked for

## Changed

- **Bookmarks and reminders belong to where they were made.** One made in a project's session
  shows in every session of that project, and only there: its marks in the list, its
  reminders coming up when an agent of that project ends a turn. One made outside projects
  shows only in its own session. Before, every session in the same folder showed it, also
  those of other projects.
- **Orphans wait under Bookmarks / Reminders** below the sessions (Open all, Delete for good):
  those of a project that is gone (deleted, or its last session closed) and those of a session
  outside projects that closed. Bookmarks kept by an older version take the project their
  session is in, once.
- **On a phone, only a tap on the input opens the keyboard**: on the cursor's row, in a coding
  agent's input box, or the keyboard button. A tap elsewhere on the terminal (to read, on a
  link, a program's click) leaves the keyboard as it was.

## Fixed

- **The reminders' pane stayed open over another session**, at the window's top left, when
  another session was shown. It closes now.
- **A restore took running sessions out of their projects**: a restore again keeps the sessions
  still running in their projects, and a restored server's bookmarks follow their panes' new
  keys.
