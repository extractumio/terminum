# v0.18.1 — An answer shown once, a compact Ask quote

A patch release: fixes and a smaller paste of an existing button (Ask), no new feature, screen
or protocol change, so the next patch after v0.18.0.

## Fixed

- **An agent's answer no longer shows twice** in a pane whose history reached tmux's
  history-limit: where tmux drops a tenth of the history at once, rows that scrolled off under a
  redrawn bottom row (a coding agent's spinner) were counted wrongly and the screen's rows were
  added to the history again.
- **The wavy line marks the text you asked about**, never its copy in the message you sent
  (after the agent reprinted its answer, the copy stood nearer).

## Changed

- **Ask's quote is compact**: `Quote #0007: «…»` on one line with your question under it, the
  quote's blank lines left out and one new line after it. Three long quotes on a phone made
  Claude Code's input box taller than the screen, and its redraw then wrote over its answer.
