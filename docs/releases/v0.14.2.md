# v0.14.2 — Marks land on their message, uploads up to 100 MB

## Changed

- **Uploads take 100 MB** (a dropped file, Upload, a pasted image), on this Mac and on a host;
  they took 20 MB. A larger file is not sent: the page says to type or paste its path instead,
  never the server's raw "length limit exceeded".

## Fixed

- **A mark's press lands on its message**: it often went to a wrong place in the history. Your
  message is found only where the agent's prompt shows it, never where an answer quotes it;
  Claude Code's "[Pasted text #3 +5 lines]" is not looked for; a mark standing for several items
  tries each; a line hidden under a widget brings the widget into view. An answer's end is never
  taken from a question's options.
- **Ask's quote numbers** count on after terminumd restarts (they began again at 1).
- **A bookmark's wavy line** stands apart from the terminal's edge and its text, and shows as soon
  as the bookmark is saved (it showed only once scrolled into view).
- **Starship's prompt** ("at 19:20 ❯︎ ") is told apart from the command you sent.
