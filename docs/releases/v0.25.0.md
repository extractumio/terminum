# v0.25.0 — sorting in Files, everything back after tmux restarts, bookmarks as a navigator

A minor release: a new feature (the Files tree sorts by name, extension or recent first),
with fixes to what comes back after tmux restarts and to the bookmarks' pane.

## New

- **Sorting in Files.** The tree's context menu has Sorting › Recent First, Alphabetical
  (the default, as Finder's names) and By Extension; folders always come first, and the
  choice is kept. terminumd sorts, so a folder of 500,000 entries still pages (Recent First:
  its first rows in about 2 s cold, later pages at once).
- **A held finger opens the tree's menu** on an iPhone (iOS Safari sends no right-click); a
  finger never drags a row.
- **[+] in the session list's head** (on a computer), left of the button that hides the list,
  opens New session's form under the head.
- **The full name and folder in a narrow window.** A tap on the session's title in the header
  slides the header's buttons aside for 3 seconds and shows the session's whole name and
  folder; ☰ opens the list.
- **The bookmarks' pane is a navigator**: the list at the left and the chosen one at the right,
  an agent's record as Markdown and your bookmark's text as the terminal shows it, as wide as
  View's window and as tall as the terminal, long lines wrapped. A bookmark's or reminder's
  "Insert into chat" is now **Quote in chat**: its text pasted quoted.

## Fixed

- **After `tmux -L terminum kill-server` or a reboot**, the sessions now come back with:
  - **their names**: a window tmux named after its program is tmux's to name again (rows read
    "2.1.310", Claude Code's version, for good), and a coding agent's conversation title is
    kept and shown on its row while a shell runs there, until an agent runs again and titles it;
  - **their Unread marks**, handed to the panes made again (they were dropped);
  - **their last 300 lines in the terminal**: on a tmux of this Mac a pane made again starts by
    printing them, then "[terminum: the lines above are from before tmux restarted]" and its
    shell, so they are in its history and survive the next restart. A host's panes still open
    them from the row's menu only.
- **A table lost its top rule** after Ask typed into a coding agent's box.
- **Dropped or uploaded files** go into a coding agent's box one path per line (spaces
  elsewhere, where a shell would run each line at Enter).
- **A finished plan stayed at 100% on its row** for good, also for the next requests: it is
  archived when the next request starts and leaves the row 10 minutes after its last update.
  The Plan pane's "Now" has a gray pill instead of a stripe along its edge.

## Verified

At release, on a clean tree: `make test` (cargo 641 + 1, the Panel's 38 and the Page's 138 unit
tests), clippy clean, the Panel's browser check (160), the Page's 1493 browser checks (Chromium
and WebKit, a wide and a phone size), `scripts/e2e_restore.py` (15), `scripts/e2e_lastlines.py`
(24), `scripts/e2e_remote.py` (32) and `scripts/security_check.sh` pass. A real
`kill-server` against a private terminumd and tmux brought back the window names, an agent's
title on its row, its Unread mark and its 300 lines in the terminal. Not verified: a real
reboot, a host's restore, a long press on a real iPhone.
