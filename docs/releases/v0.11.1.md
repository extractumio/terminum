# v0.11.1 — Background work counts for 10 minutes at most

## Changed

- **Background work alone keeps a session working for 10 minutes at most.** Since v0.11.0 a
  shell, a monitor or an agent still running in the background (Claude Code's footer counts
  it: "1 shell, 1 monitor") keeps a session working after its main agent finished. One that
  never ends (a dev server, a watcher) no longer holds it forever:
  - without terminum's hooks, a session the footer's count alone keeps working counts as done
    10 minutes after that began, and its turn ends 10 s later; when the agent works again,
    the 10 minutes start over;
  - with hooks, a turn ends at its Stop, or, while the footer still counts, 10 minutes after
    the Stop at the latest.

  The agent's own work (its spinner, "Waiting for N background agents to finish") has no limit.
