# v0.18.0 — A phone-first page, menus for groups, quotes that stay marked

A minor release: it adds features (the phone's folded buttons and sheets, the input's touch
menu, menus for projects and session groups, zoom in View, asked quotes kept and marked) and
changes the page's protocol (`asked` and `askedmarks`, the panel's `fb-zoom` and `fb-root`), so
the next minor after v0.17.0.

## Added

- **Projects and session groups have menus**: a right click on a project's or a tmux session's
  group header, or its ⋯, opens a menu as a row's: New Session, Rename (a project), Move to the
  Top, Up, Down, to the Bottom, Ungroup (a project), and Close Project or Close Group, which asks
  first and then closes each of its sessions.
- **Asked quotes stay marked**: text you asked the agent about (Ask's Add to Chat or Send to
  Agent) keeps a light blue wavy line down its left in the terminal, as a reminder's quote has;
  terminum keeps them per session, so every page of that session shows them.
- **Zoom in View**: a click on a picture opens it full screen (pinch, wheel, drag, a double click
  for 1:1, −, +, Fit); the checkerboard is behind the picture alone, so its transparent parts
  show as such.
- **The phone's buttons fold**: every floating button but the keyboard's two folds into one ⟨…⟩
  at the terminal's top right; a tap unfolds them for 3 s. They all hide while a finger scrolls.
- **Sheets on a phone**: every dialog, pane and menu opens centered and modal inside the visible
  screen, over a shade that closes it.
- **The input's touch menu**: on a phone a finger held still on the input and lifted opens Cut,
  Clear, Paste, Copy, Undo and Redo, each when it applies; a quick swipe right is →, left is Undo;
  a double tap selects a word in an agent's input box.
- **Docked Files' width** follows a drag of its border, remembered in this browser.

## Changed

- **Windows fly out of their button**: a menu, pane or dialog grows from what opened it, out of a
  blur on a spring, its rows coming up one after another, and goes back into it fast; every color
  change takes a moment. Nothing moves with Reduce Motion.
- **The session list**: no drag grip (a row or a header is dragged itself, a dashed outline where
  it lands); a row's Clone shows under the pointer (always on a touch screen), and a pane listed
  under its window has none; a group's buttons show under the pointer in the place of its count,
  which lines up with the rows' programs; an unread session is hatched with pale lines.
- **The header's second line** is the folder with a Copy of its full path, then tmux's
  session:window, then what runs there.
- **The terminal re-flows** lines to the browser's width when the pane's width differs (a split,
  another screen), at the chosen text size; the split-window banner is gone.
- **View**: its head, path bar and tabs stand on one grid with Reveal beside the tabs; Reveal in
  tree expands the file's folders and is offered only for a file under the folder Files shows;
  at full width View has no Terminal key (its × closes it).
- **The look**: one style for each kind of control, the instrument-panel material (near-square
  corners, matte keys that sink when pressed, a grain on the chrome), the phone header on an 8 px
  grid, and the sent history and reminders as modal panes.

## Fixed

- **A table no longer turns back into text** a second after it was drawn: a fold of a tool's
  output gives way to rendered output (a table, a diff's table, Markdown, a diagram).
- **A tool's output folds at once** once it scrolled into the history, while the agent's next
  line is still on the screen; it stayed open until that line scrolled off too.
- **On a phone**: Paste reads the clipboard once (Safari asked twice), an empty paste sends
  nothing and says so, a press on ⟨…⟩ puts the keyboard away, every banner has an ×, the reminders'
  bell stands still, and iOS's own menu no longer covers a selection (the page's Copy, [+] and
  Ask stand at the top right).
- **History at tmux's limit** keeps its numbering instead of starting anew; busybox and BSD
  hosts are read correctly; a pane that leaves a project at once leaves it for good.
- **A half-drawn table** seen over ssh waits for the rest instead of staying text.
