# v0.26.0 — agents' records make their own room, the list's foot as a toggle, a full-tree review

A minor release: coding agents' bookmarks and reminders get limits of their own and make room
instead of being refused, the session list's foot unfolds on a click, and the fixes of a review of
the whole tree. Nothing new to install; `terminum upgrade` brings it.

## New

- **Agents' records make their own room.** A coding agent's bookmarks and reminders
  (add_notes_for_user) were refused once a project held 50 of them together, and only the agent
  was told. Now each project (or a session outside projects) keeps 50 of agents' bookmarks and
  20 of their open reminders: past them the oldest of that kind go and the new ones are kept.
- **A banner says so.** When the next agent turn of that project ends, every page shows
  "Agents' bookmarks of <project> are over 50: the oldest are deleted automatically. Review
  them." over the terminal, with Open bookmarks (or Open reminders) and ×, which closes it on
  every page.
- **Resolved reminders go after 30 days** and count in no limit. Your own bookmarks (300) and
  open reminders (100) have limits of their own, still refused with why and never deleted for
  room.
- **The list's foot is a toggle.** Its row of icons now has ▸: a click unfolds New session,
  Bookmarks / Reminders, Add host and Attach tmux sessions (▾), and they stay until you click it
  again; the pointer passing over it no longer unfolds it. The page remembers which.

## Changed

- A tmux session's group's menu says **New Window** (it said New Session): it makes a window
  in that session, as Clone. A project's menu keeps New Session.
- The bookmarks' floating button is an orange bookmark on the plain buttons' fill, as the
  reminders' bell (it was dark on an orange blob).

## Fixed

- **A table in a coding agent's long output** showed its lines again under the drawn table
  once the output was folded; they stay hidden now. A table whose rows were cut short (`cut -c`)
  stays text instead of a head alone over its rows.

From a review of the whole tree:

- **Typing and pasting reach the pane they were meant for:** queued keys no longer go to an equal
  pane number on another tmux server; a clipboard read or an image still being converted when you
  switch panes no longer pastes into the new one; confirming Close on a quick shell closes the one
  you were asked about.
- **Enter on Cancel or Close in the Files panel's dialogs** no longer moves files to the Trash.
- **Sent history hides a quoted secret value whole** (`--password 'two words'`), and an
  apostrophe earlier in a prompt no longer hides the rest of it.
- **Coding agents' hooks:** a late event of an ended session no longer erases the new session's
  state and plan; `terminum hooks remove` keeps your own commands that share an entry with
  terminum's; deleting a task no longer renames the next one.
- **Restore and hosts:** a failed checkpoint is retried; window names and folders with `#S` stay
  literal; a host whose recipe changed, was switched off or became invalid lets go of its Files
  connection; settings edited by hand resync hosts.
- **Search and the cursor with Unicode:** rows whose text grows when lowercased (İ) keep their
  hits; a tap on a shell's line moves over emoji by characters.
- **The YAML viewer** shows explicit indentation and escaped line ends as YAML means them and
  reports an undefined alias; a late answer no longer reopens folders after Collapse all.
- **The `terminum` command:** a reply that arrived whole is no longer reported as unavailable; a
  timed-out helper command ends its whole process group; `terminum import` refuses a settings
  file of the wrong shape instead of replacing it.
- **Projects:** a projects file of the wrong shape is set aside before anything writes over it.
