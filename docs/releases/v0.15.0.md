# v0.15.0 — Dialogs of one kind, a better iPhone keyboard

## Changed

- **Every dialog looks and works alike** (the Page's and the Files panel's, and the session
  list's New session, Add host and Attach forms): a title and a × in the corner (as Esc), fields
  in the terminal's font at its size, Cancel then the primary button. The primary takes the color
  of what opened it: Ask blue, a bookmark orange, a reminder pink, an irreversible action red. A
  confirmation's question is its title.

## Fixed

- **Typing on an iPhone**: the hidden field keeps the word being typed, so iOS aims its keys by
  it (every letter was aimed as a word's first); a change iOS makes to the word reaches the
  terminal as backspaces and the new text.
- **The keyboard's space-bar trackpad** moves the cursor left and right (it moved nothing).
- **Opening a session** stays on its last line while widgets, images, fonts and a phone's list
  settle (it often stopped short); scrolling, a press, typing or a selection lets it go.
- **The view no longer jumps on a phone** as the screen redraws while the keyboard is up.
