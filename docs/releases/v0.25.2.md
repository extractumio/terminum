# v0.25.2 — Bookmarks as a mail list, Reminders the same window, a steady input box

A patch release: changes to windows that are already there (how Bookmarks and Reminders show
their entries, the windows' type, Markdown's look) and one fix; nothing new to install or
upgrade.

## Changed

- **Bookmarks read as a mail list.** The window has the list on top, each entry its title and,
  under it, its text's start on one line (an agent's without its Markdown's marks), where and
  when at the right; the chosen entry's content under the list. Drag the line between them to
  share the height, a double click halves it. A note longer than its title line shows whole
  above the text. The bar over the terminal and the rail's tips show the same titles.
- **Reminders open the same window**, with the open reminders only, those up first, and the
  same actions and keys: Show in terminal (Enter), Resolve (Delete, with Undo for 5 seconds),
  Quote in chat. Its own Open, Close and Delete are gone.
- **The floating windows' text is the chat's**: Bookmarks, Reminders, Sent, Plan and Open all
  use the terminal's font at its size, line height 1.5; their heads, times and keys stay the
  interface's.
- **Markdown looks one way on every screen**: the terminal's widgets, Bookmarks, Reminders, the
  sent history and Open all share one style. Links take the theme's link color with a faint
  underline, never the browser's blue or visited purple.
- The floating windows are placed again when a banner shows or goes.
- The README shows terminum on a monitor, a laptop and a phone, linked to
  [terminum.app](https://terminum.app/); the commercial license names terminum.

## Fixed

- **A Claude Code session's input box twitched** at every redraw of its spinner (every 100 ms):
  a redraw that reached tmux in two parts was captured between them, so the page lost the input
  box for a frame. terminumd now captures once the pane has been quiet for 6 ms, 33 ms after
  the first notice at most.
